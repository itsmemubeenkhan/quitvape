1. Information We Collect

QuitVape collects the following information to provide its services:

  • Profile Information: Name, email address, weight, and height (provided by you during onboarding)
  • Usage Data: Your quit date, cravings logged, achievements, and app interactions
  • Purchase Information: Subscription purchase history (processed by Google Play Billing)
  • Device Information: Device identifiers for app functionality and analytics

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To track your quit-vaping progress and show personalized statistics
  • To provide AI coaching responses tailored to your journey
  • To process subscription purchases via Google Play Billing
  • To improve app functionality and user experience

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store your personal information. AI coaching requests are sent to our AI provider to generate responses.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with:

  • Google Play Billing: To process subscription purchases
  • AI Service Provider: To generate coaching responses (messages you send to the AI coach)

5. Data Security

All data transmitted is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

6. Your Rights

You can delete all your data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

7. Children's Privacy

QuitVape is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
